# 0003. Carry the Sequoyah heritage as brand depth

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Revised:** 2026-09-29 (Touchstone is one workshop of three; no outreach, decided 2026-06-19)

## Context

SEQTEK is short for Sequoyah Technologies, named for the Cherokee polymath whose syllabary gave his nation
near-universal literacy within a generation. The brand research (private content repo) found that story-based
differentiation ranks second among the priorities of high-growth professional-services firms, and that no comparable
regional firm grounds its brand in an origin story.

## Options considered

- **Drop the heritage** — a coined mark with no story.
- **Mention it in passing** — a footnote on the story page.
- **Layered depth** — SEQTEK stays the surface mark, and the story sits one click in.

## Decision

Layered depth. SEQTEK is the mark everywhere. One homepage sentence carries the depth signal and links to
`/our-story`, which carries a 200–400 word origin narrative and a one-line cultural acknowledgement that SEQTEK is a
non-tribal firm honouring Sequoyah's legacy. The connecting thread, _systems that scale what people can do_, is also the
thesis of the Touchstone workshop. The draft copy is in the private content repo. There is no Cherokee Nation outreach.

## Consequences

- A differentiator the regional field does not use, and a hook for press and podcasts.
- The story page and the acknowledgement need careful stewardship; a sloppy execution is worse than none.
- A residual risk that the framing is read as appropriation; the acknowledgement is the mitigation.

## Revisit when

A credible critique comes from the Cherokee community, the firm repositions away from its Oklahoma roots, or the
Touchstone workshop is retired.
